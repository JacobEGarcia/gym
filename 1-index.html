ABSTRACT

Three benchmark rigs, one idea: pump energy, then catch. Each controller measures the system's total energy with mj_energyPos/mj_energyVel and drives it toward the upright value — then hands off to a local stabilizer near the top. Pendulum and cart-pole balance for keeps; the acrobot (one elbow motor, two links) swings to full height and fights to stay — the honest hard one. Physics by MuJoCo WebAssembly at 1× speed.
